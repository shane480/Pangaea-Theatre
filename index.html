The Headliner

Comedy's Original Outsider

Adam Carolla is best known as a comedian, actor, radio personality, television host and NY Times best-selling author. He currently hosts The Adam Carolla Show, which holds the Guinness Book of World Records for "most downloaded podcast."

Starting from humble beginnings — digging ditches and swinging hammers on construction sites throughout Southern California — a fateful meeting with Jimmy Kimmel put him on the path to comedy stardom via KROQ and the nationally syndicated Loveline with Dr. Drew Pinsky.

Don't miss this rare chance to see the podcasting king deliver his trademark brand of common-sense comedy — completely unfiltered and live on stage.

Get Ready to Laugh

Get ready to laugh until your sides hurt because Adam Carolla is coming to Pangaea Theatre! This stand-up comedy show, headlined by the legendary comedian, radio personality, and podcasting king, promises an evening of unfiltered humor and razor-sharp wit.

Carolla is known for his work on "Loveline," "The Man Show," and "The Adam Carolla Show" podcast. His unique comedic style combines observational humor with biting social commentary that leaves no sacred cow untouched.
